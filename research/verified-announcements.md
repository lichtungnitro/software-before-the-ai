# Primary-source LLM integration announcements

These announcements document product direction or rollout, **not** the last qualifying pre-LLM binary. Do not substitute announcement dates for version numbers.

## Microsoft 365 Copilot
- Announcement: 2023-03-16
- Affected product family: Microsoft 365 (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams)
- Primary source: https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2023/03/16/introducing-microsoft-365-copilot-your-copilot-for-work/
- Important caveat: Initially tested with select commercial customers. Consumer releases, editions, and rollout dates require separate verification.
- Historical version: unknown

## Google Gemini mobile integration
- Announcement: 2024-02-08
- Affected product family: Google app on iOS and Gemini/Assistant experiences on Android
- Primary source: https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/bard-gemini-advanced-app/
- Important caveat: iOS Google app rollout was gradual, Android Gemini was a separate experience; this does not date the first LLM integration in every Google product.
- Historical version: unknown

## Research rule
A qualifying record needs evidence of the **last release without integrated generative AI**, with platform, edition, region, rollout conditions, and a trustworthy download URL if available. Mark server-side/flagged rollouts ambiguous if a version alone cannot guarantee no LLM features.
